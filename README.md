# Aura Bridal

Aura Bridal is an elevated, modern bridal atelier web application handcrafted for brides seeking refined simplicity, timeless gowns, luminous jewelry, and artisanal wedding essentials.

## Features

- **Modern Bridal Collection**: Minimalist aesthetic showcasing handcrafted gowns, cathedral veils, fine pearl jewelry, satin pumps, and clutches.
- **Curated Category Navigation**: Quick filtering across Gowns & Dresses, Fine Jewelry, Veils & Combs, and Bridal Shoes.
- **Interactive Catalogue**:
  - Live real-time search across titles and descriptions
  - Multi-category filtering and price tier breakdown (Under $500, $500–$1,500, Over $1,500)
  - Sorting by Featured, Price (Low to High / High to Low), and Highest Rated
  - Filter chips / active tags with individual dismissal and clear-all actions
- **Quick View**: Full product modal with high-resolution preview, complete atelier descriptions, and direct bag addition.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison**: Compare up to 3 selected pieces with comparison table analyzing preview images, prices, categories, aesthetic vibe, silhouettes, and ratings.
- **Saved Favorites (Wishlist)**: Persistent wishlist drawer with "Move to Bag" actions.
- **Interactive Style Match Quiz**:
  - 3-step questionnaire addressing dream venue, silhouette preference, and budget
  - Dynamic curation algorithm recommending matched gowns and accessories
- **Shopping Bag & Dynamic Free Shipping**:
  - Slide-in cart drawer with quantity adjustments
  - Real-time animated progress bar indicating distance to Free Worldwide Shipping ($500 threshold)
- **Multi-Step Checkout**:
  - Step 1: Shipping address details
  - Step 2: Payment details
  - Step 3: Order review with breakdown of items, subtotal, shipping, and grand total
- **Responsive & Accessible**: Clean design on desktop and mobile devices.

## Tech Stack

- **Framework**: React 19 + TypeScript
- **Bundler & Dev Server**: Vite
- **Typography**: Cormorant Garamond & Montserrat
- **Icons**: Minimalist inline SVG & Lucide
